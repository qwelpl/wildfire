    How to play

    Spell words by sliding across letters that touch, sideways or diagonally. Three letters or more.

    Every word has to use at least one tile that's on fire. The rest of its tiles catch.

    Burn the whole board in as few words as you can. Par is the best we found.

    Burned tiles can be used again. Drag and let go to play a word. Or tap tiles one by one, then tap the word above the board.
